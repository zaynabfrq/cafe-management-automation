## Overview

Cafe Management & CRM Automation Dashboard is an open-source project focused on making everyday café operations easier to manage through a modern, lightweight interface.

## Preview

![Cafe Management Dashboard](./cafe-dashboard.png)

The project explores how small hospitality businesses can replace scattered manual processes with a centralized digital workflow for reservations, customer management, booking operations, and business analytics.

The project is currently in early development and is being actively improved as an open-source learning and development project.

## Why This Project?

Many small cafés rely on a combination of notebooks, spreadsheets, messaging apps, and disconnected tools to manage reservations and customer information.

This project explores a simpler approach: bringing important operational workflows into a single dashboard that can be adapted to the needs of small hospitality businesses.

## Current Features

### Reservation Management
- Interactive reservation table
- Customer information tracking
- Reservation status management
- Easy visibility into upcoming bookings

### Booking Workflow
- Dynamic booking interface
- Walk-in and priority guest logging
- Quick booking updates
- Structured reservation workflow

### Business Analytics
- Revenue tracking
- Table occupancy metrics
- Daily performance indicators
- KPI-focused dashboard

## Technology

- **React** — Frontend application
- **Tailwind CSS** — Interface styling
- **Lucide React** — Icons and interface components

## Project Goals

The long-term goal is to develop the project into a reusable open-source foundation for small cafés and hospitality businesses.

Planned areas of development include:

- Improved reservation workflows
- Customer relationship management
- Automated customer follow-ups
- Business reporting and analytics
- Additional hospitality integrations
- More configurable automation workflows
- Improved documentation and developer experience
